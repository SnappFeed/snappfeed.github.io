Support

Help and contact information for the SnappFeed iOS and Android applications.

Contact support

For technical support, bug reports, or privacy questions, email:

zakezfayaz@outlook.com

Please include your platform (iOS or Android), app version, device model, and a short description of the problem. Do not send private photos or sensitive location information.

Managing permissions

Camera, Photos/Media, and Location permissions can be managed from your device settings. Disabling a permission may disable only the feature that requires it.

Deleting local data

You can delete individual memories within the app. Uninstalling SnappFeed will normally remove data stored in the app's private local storage. Copies may remain in device or cloud backups according to your backup settings.
